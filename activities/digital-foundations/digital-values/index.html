1. Analogue and digital values

Analogue values vary continuously. Digital values are discrete: only particular states or levels are recognised. The original tutor illustrated this by mapping a range of analogue voltages onto logic 0 and logic 1.

The symbols 0 and 1 do not always mean voltage. The original tutor used examples from logic, memory, optics and storage to show that two distinguishable physical states can represent one bit.

3. Bits and bytes
