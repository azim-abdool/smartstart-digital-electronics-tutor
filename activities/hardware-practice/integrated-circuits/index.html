What is an integrated circuit?

The original tutor described an IC as a semiconductor crystal containing interconnected electronic components implemented with transistors.

Inside the chip

An integrated circuit combines many components on one piece of semiconductor material. The external package protects the chip and makes its terminals accessible.

Three characteristics were emphasised in the original activity: transistor technology, package, and scale of integration.
