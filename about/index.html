About the reconstruction

The SmartStart Digital Electronics Tutor was originally developed as a Flash-based learning aid. This edition preserves its instructional content and interactions by rebuilding them with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG.

Recovery principles

  • Retain the original topics, examples and exercise logic wherever practical.
  • Replace obsolete Flash controls with accessible web controls.
  • Redraw assets only when direct recovery is impractical.
  • Remove broken external dependencies such as the EVITA executable launcher.
  • Clearly identify reconstructed, modified and newly created elements.

Branding and credit

The interface has been adapted for the IEEE EDS Trinidad and Tobago Chapter. This does not imply that IEEE authored the original tutor. Original authorship and source credits will be retained as they are recovered and verified.
